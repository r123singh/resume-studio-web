Quickstart

Docs

1. Install

Download for Windows, extract, and run Resume Studio.exe. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

2. Choose a model

Open Settings. You can start immediately, or bring your own key.

  • NVIDIA NIM — included. No key to paste.
  • Ollama — local and free. Install Ollama, pull a model, and pick it in Settings. Prompts stay on your machine.
  • OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or Amazon Bedrock — optional. Keys stay encrypted on this PC.

3. Open a workspace

Click Open workspace folder. Resume Studio creates base-resume.md, job-preferences.md, resumes/, and apply-kits/ if they are missing. Paste your master resume into base-resume.md and save (Ctrl+S).

4. Tailor from a JD

In the AI panel, open Tailor from JD. Enter company and role, paste the job description, and generate. The draft lands at resumes/company--role.md. Large writes show a diff first — nothing is saved until you accept it.

5. Apply kit, tracker, and hunt

Apply kit writes apply-kits/company--role/ with the resume PDF, cover letter, form snippets, and a checklist, and logs the row in applications.csv.

Tracker filters those rows. Mark applied or skipped, and open the job URL or the kit folder.

Hunt searches RemoteOK and Remotive, ranks postings against job-preferences.md, and can prepare a selected job into a tailored resume plus kit.

6. Mail inbox

Open Mail (Ctrl+Shift+L). Fetch over IMAP (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, Zoho, iCloud, or a custom host), import an .eml, or paste a forwarded JD. The app password is stored encrypted and is not sent to a model.

Pick a job, then Send reply or Tailor + kit. Send uses SMTP and asks you to confirm the recipient. New mail is a blank message for a follow-up that is not tied to an inbox job. You can attach resume.pdf after the kit exists.

7. WhatsApp desk

Open WhatsApp (Ctrl+Shift+M) and paste the recruiter thread. Parsing is local: name, phone, company, role, and a short ping meant to be read on a phone.

Open WhatsApp launches wa.me with the ping filled in. Attach the PDF from the apply kit yourself — the link cannot attach files. Resume Studio does not send the message.

8. Evidence, agent, and interview prep

  • Evidence (Ctrl+Shift+E) — research a job URL (opt in first) or paste the posting. Suggested bullets cite the snippets they came from. Accept them one by one.
  • Agent mode — in Settings, the model can list, read, and search your workspace, then propose an edit. The file changes only after you accept the diff.
  • Interview prep — from an open tailored resume or a Tracker row, write interview-prep/company--role.md.
  • Recruiter Lens — Edit, ATS, and Recruiter scan modes, plus a six-second skim preview.
  • Role variants — Frontend, Backend, and Product drafts under variants/.

Tips

  • The model is instructed to use only facts already in your resume. It should not invent employers, titles, dates, or metrics.
  • Ctrl+Shift+P opens the command palette. Ctrl+P jumps to a file.
  • You can point the app at an existing job-search folder.
  • If Windows SmartScreen warns, choose More info → Run anyway. This build is unsigned.
