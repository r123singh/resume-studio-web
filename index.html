Apply kit and tracker

Job hunt

Search RemoteOK and Remotive, rank them against your preferences, then prepare the ones you pick.

Mail inbox

Fetch Gmail or IMAP, import an .eml, or paste a JD. Send the reply over SMTP when you confirm.

WhatsApp desk

Paste a recruiter thread, get a short phone ping, and open WhatsApp with the PDF ready to attach.

Evidence and agent

Cite the posting behind each bullet, or let agent mode read your files and propose a diff you accept.
