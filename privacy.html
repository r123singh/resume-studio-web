Privacy

Resume Studio is a desktop app. Your resumes stay in the folder you open. API keys and mail app passwords are stored locally with Electron safeStorage when available.

What leaves your machine

  • Tailor, chat edit, evidence tailor, interview prep, and optional “polish with AI” send resume text and job text to the model provider you selected: NVIDIA, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or Amazon Bedrock.
  • Ollama talks to localhost. Those prompts are not sent to a cloud model.
  • Job research fetches a posting only after you turn web research on. Pasted job text is processed locally.
  • Mail fetch and send talk to the mail host you configure (IMAP and SMTP). Message bodies are not sent to a model unless you choose polish with AI. Nothing is sent until you confirm the recipient.
  • WhatsApp paste stays on this PC unless you polish with AI. Open WhatsApp hands the ping to wa.me. You send it yourself.

What we do not do

  • No auto-submit to job boards or ATS forms.
  • No Resume Studio cloud account in this public release. There is no hosted copy of your resumes.
  • No selling of resume data.
  • This marketing site may use standard hosting logs from the platform (Vercel).
