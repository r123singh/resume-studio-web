Privacy

Resume Studio is a desktop app. Your resumes stay in the folder you open. API keys are stored locally with Electron safeStorage when available.

What leaves your machine

  • When you run Tailor or Edit, resume text and job descriptions are sent to the LLM provider you chose (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini) using your key.
  • We do not operate a Resume Studio account system or store your resumes in our cloud.

What we do not do

  • No auto-submit to job boards or ATS forms.
  • No selling of resume data.
  • This marketing site may use standard hosting analytics from the platform (Vercel).
